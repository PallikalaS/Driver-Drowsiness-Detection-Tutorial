[1] Real-Time Driver Drowsiness Detection System Based on PERCLOS and Grayscale Image Processing

Authors
Jun-Juh Yan, Hang-Hong Kuo, Ying-Fan Lin, Teh-Lu Liao
Date
2016
Source
2016 International Symposium on Computer, Consumer and Control (IS3C), IEEE, pp. 243–246. doi:10.1109/IS3C.2016.72
Synopsis
Presents a real-time system that locates the driver's face in grayscale frames, finds the eyes with a small template, and builds a fatigue model for each driver from PERCLOS. The system monitors the driver continuously and issues an alert once fatigue is detected.
Reliability
Peer-reviewed IEEE conference paper by university researchers, including a faculty member at National Cheng Kung University. A short paper, so the evaluation is limited in scope.

[2] Driver Drowsiness Classification Based on Eye Blink and Head Movement Features Using the k-NN Algorithm

Authors
Mariella Dreißig, Mohamed Hedi Baccour, Tim Schäck, Enkelejda Kasneci
Date
2020
Source
2020 IEEE Symposium Series on Computational Intelligence (SSCI), pp. 889–896. doi:10.1109/SSCI47803.2020.9308133. Free preprint: arXiv:2009.13276
Synopsis
Extends in-vehicle drowsiness detection by using signals from a driver monitoring camera instead of only steering and lane data. Develops a k-Nearest Neighbor based feature-selection method over eye-blink and head-movement features, and shows which feature sets best capture how sleepiness changes blink behavior and head motion.
Reliability
Peer-reviewed IEEE symposium paper. The senior author is a university professor who leads a human-computer interaction research group.
